Module 5 Training

8 Practical Front Desk Scenarios

Exact operational guidance from the Rooming Volunteer Notebook on what to do and what NOT to do.

Scenario 1

Missing Hotel Reservation

"A delegate arrives at the Rooming Desk and says the hotel cannot find their reservation."

What to Do:
  • Listen carefully to delegate
  • Remain calm & reassuring
  • Gather rooming details
  • Inform your Keyman
  • Wait for verification/direction
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT promise a room immediately
  • Do NOT make a new booking yourself
  • Do NOT argue with delegate or hotel
Scenario 2

Incorrect Room Assignment

"A delegate reports that the room assigned does not match their confirmed arrangement."

What to Do:
  • Verify the details provided
  • Record concern accurately
  • Inform Keyman / Hotel Coordinator
  • Assist while assignment is checked
  • Follow confirmed resolution
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT change assignment yourself
  • Do NOT give unverified info
  • Do NOT send delegate elsewhere without coordination
Scenario 3

Tired & Frustrated Delegate

"After a long trip, a delegate arrives tired and upset because of a lodging problem."

What to Do:
  • Listen patiently
  • Speak calmly and kindly
  • Acknowledge the concern
  • Reassure assistance is being sought
  • Inform Keyman promptly
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT argue or be defensive
  • Do NOT dismiss their frustration
  • Do NOT make promises you cannot confirm
Scenario 4

Hotel Reports a Rooming Discrepancy

"Hotel personnel inform you that their room assignment list does not match Rooming Department info."

What to Do:
  • Listen carefully to hotel staff
  • Note specific discrepancy
  • Inform Keyman / Hotel Coordinator
  • Wait while records are verified
  • Follow confirmed arrangement
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT decide which list is right
  • Do NOT change assignments yourself
  • Do NOT negotiate independently with hotel
Scenario 5

Delegate Requests a Room Change

"A delegate asks to transfer to another room or hotel because of personal preference."

What to Do:
  • Listen respectfully
  • Ask for the reason for change
  • Inform Keyman
  • Verify if approved adjustment exists
  • Follow given direction
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT promise room changes
  • Do NOT contact other hotels alone
  • Do NOT alter room records yourself
Scenario 6

Urgent Lodging Concern (Safety)

"A delegate reports a serious concern with lodging affecting safety or habitability."

What to Do:
  • Take concern seriously
  • Gather essential details
  • Inform Keyman immediately
  • Coordinate with Hotel Coordinator
  • Stay available until referred
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT minimize the concern
  • Do NOT attempt major fixes alone
  • Do NOT delay reporting urgent issues
Scenario 7

Last-Minute Accommodation Need

"A delegate arrives without confirmed lodging and asks the Rooming Department for assistance."

What to Do:
  • Welcome the delegate calmly
  • Gather necessary details
  • Inform your Keyman
  • Follow established rooming process
  • Provide only confirmed info
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT guarantee accommodation
  • Do NOT arrange lodging independently
  • Do NOT send them to hotels without direction
Scenario 8

Confidential Rooming Information

"Someone asks you for another delegate's hotel, room number, or accommodation details."

What to Do:
  • Protect delegate privacy
  • Politely decline giving private details
  • Refer request to Keyman
  • Keep records physical & secure
What NOT to Do:
  • Do NOT disclose room numbers casually
  • Do NOT show room lists to anyone
  • Do NOT discuss room details with peers

Key Safeguards & Christian Conduct

What every Rooming Volunteer MUST ALWAYS and NEVER do.

App Confidentiality & Usage Disclaimer
Internal Volunteer Use Only

This application is intended strictly and exclusively for appointed IC 2026 Local Rooming Volunteers. The internal protocols, room rates, and lines of reporting contained here are confidential.

STRICT RULE: This app can be used only for Local Rooming volunteers and cannot be shared with anyone.
ALWAYS
  • Follow approved Rooming procedures and instructions from your Keyman.
  • Give only verified and accurate information; verify with Keyman when unsure.
  • Stay within your assigned responsibilities; you are support, not decision-makers.
  • Protect personal & rooming information (Scenario 8). Keep lists away from public eyes.
  • Follow your assigned line of oversight (Volunteer → Keyman → Asst. Overseer).
  • Watch for safety hazards (overcrowding, trip hazards, balcony tampering).
  • Show Christian kindness & mildness even when delegates are stressed (Colossians 3:12).
NEVER
  • NEVER change a room assignment on your own under any circumstance.
  • NEVER promise early check-in, late checkout, room upgrades, or free beds.
  • NEVER commit the hotel or organization to fees, waivers, or financial adjustments.
  • NEVER disclose confidential delegate information or show rooming sheets to third parties.
  • NEVER share or forward this application or its contents to anyone outside the Local Rooming team.
  • NEVER negotiate independently with hotel management or front office staff.
  • NEVER argue with delegates or hotel personnel.
